Compass carving and orientation

The ring, four cardinal letters, radial rules and long divided needle follow the CIA compass photograph. The lodestone sits along the needle’s extension.

Elonka’s reference reports the engraved N at approximately 220°; its exact bearing is not established. The needle is drawn at its photographed angle to that N, rather than being made to point at the letter N. Exact entrance coordinates and bearings remain estimates.

About the sculpture

Dedicated on 3 November 1990 in the north-west corner of the New Headquarters Building courtyard. A petrified tree holds an S-shaped copper screen “like a waving flag”, as Jim Gillogly put it.

The page nearest the tree carries the ciphertext. The far page carries a KRYPTOS-keyed Vigenère tableau, cut from the other side so it reads correctly only from behind. Hover any letter to see where it sits in the code.

A bubbling pool sits at the base, with red and green granite and white quartz around it. Sanborn's Morse-code slabs and compass rose with a lodestone stand at the building entrance and are included in the expanded, separately switchable site reconstruction.

K4 · 97 letters · publicly unsolved
Sanborn's clues: positions 22–25 read EAST, 26–34 NORTHEAST, 64–69 BERLIN and 70–74 CLOCK. His archive, including the solution, was auctioned in November 2025; the plaintext hasn't been published.
Quirks to look for
  • Raised Y, A and R on the top line of the lower-left panel (ENDYAHROHN…).
  • An extra L in tableau row N, which makes H-I-L-L run down the last column. Try the “HILL column” view.
  • Sanborn left out an S near the end of K2, so the carved text decrypts to “ID BY ROWS”; in 2006 he confirmed it should read “X LAYER TWO”. Hover the end of row 14.
  • Deliberate misspellings in the solutions: IQLUSION, UNDERGRUUND, DESPARATLY.
About this model

A reconstruction for exploring, not a survey. The text and its line layout are exact (869 cipher characters, 867 tableau letters). Overall size follows published figures (about 12 ft high and 20 ft of 13 mm copper along the curve). The direction and depth of the S are matched to a photo from the courtyard; letter size and the pool and rock placement are estimates from photos and descriptions.

Reference photos: Jim Gillogly's Kryptos Pix. Letterforms: Liberation Sans Bold (SIL Open Font License), a stand-in for Sanborn's letters; the Letters panel can recut them from a replica font file such as Gary Phillips' "Kryptos Sculpture". Rendering: three.js (MIT).

The red foreground stone follows its broken outline and recesses in Jim Gillogly’s 1999 photographs; cropped surface details are credited to Jim Gillogly, Kryptos Pix.
